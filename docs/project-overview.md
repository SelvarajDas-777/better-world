# Better World — Project Overview

## 1. Project Title

**Better World — A Community Help and Assistance Platform**

## 2. Introduction

Better World is a community-focused mobile application designed to connect people who need assistance with people who are willing to help. The application provides a structured platform where users can either request help or offer their assistance to others.

The primary idea is to make community support more accessible, organized, and easier to initiate through a simple digital platform.

## 3. Problem Statement

People may sometimes require assistance with everyday activities, urgent situations, transportation, essential supplies, or other forms of support. In many situations, finding a suitable person nearby who is willing and able to help can be difficult.

Existing methods such as asking friends, family members, neighbors, or posting on social media may not provide a structured way to connect the person requiring assistance with an appropriate helper.

Better World aims to provide a dedicated platform for organizing these community assistance requests.

## 4. Proposed Solution

Better World provides two primary user pathways:

- **Need Help** — for users who require assistance.
- **I Can Help** — for users who are willing to provide assistance.

Users can access these functions after authentication. Help requests can contain relevant information about the assistance required, allowing potential helpers to understand the request and respond appropriately.

The application also provides an **Emergency** option intended to provide quick access to emergency-related assistance.

## 5. Objectives

The main objectives of Better World are:

1. To create a platform for requesting and offering community assistance.
2. To connect people who need help with potential helpers.
3. To provide a simple and accessible user interface.
4. To provide an emergency assistance pathway.
5. To organize help requests in a structured manner.
6. To provide a foundation for future location-based and real-time assistance features.
7. To encourage community participation and mutual support.
8. To create a "Better World" by increasing the trust between people

## 6. Target Users

The application is intended for:

- People who require assistance with everyday activities.
- Individuals willing to volunteer or assist others.
- Community groups and local volunteers.
- Users requiring quick access to emergency-related assistance.

## 7. Core Features

### 7.1 Welcome Screen

The introductory screen presents the purpose of the application and provides the primary actions:

- Need Help
- I Can Help
- Emergency

### 7.2 User Authentication

Users can create an account and log in to access the application's main features.

### 7.3 Need Help

Users can create a help request by providing relevant details about the assistance they require.

### 7.4 I Can Help

Users who are willing to assist others can browse or respond to suitable help requests.

### 7.5 Emergency Assistance

The application provides a dedicated emergency pathway for situations requiring immediate attention.

### 7.6 User Profile

Users can maintain basic information associated with their account and manage their participation in the platform.

### 7.7 Help Request Management

The system can maintain the status of help requests, allowing requests to progress from creation to response and completion.

## 10. Project Scope

The initial scope focuses on developing a functional prototype with user authentication, help-request functionality, assistance participation, emergency access, and a user-friendly interface.

Future development may extend the application with real-time communication, location-based matching, notifications, user verification, trust/reputation mechanisms, and AI-assisted support.

## 11. Expected Outcome

The expected outcome is a functional community assistance application that provides a structured way for people to request help and for willing individuals to offer assistance.

The project will also demonstrate the use of modern mobile application development practices, UI/UX design, version control, and incremental software development.
